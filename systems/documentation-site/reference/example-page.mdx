The **Timesheets** page is where a manager's week lives: every clocked entry across the crew, laid out by day, with totals that already exclude unpaid breaks and lunch. From here you review, fix, approve, and export.

<Shot src="/docs-img/timesheets/timesheets.png" device="desktop" alt="Weekly timesheet grid with employees as rows and days as columns" caption="Timesheets — the weekly grid. Weekend columns are tinted; totals exclude unpaid breaks." />

## Reading the grid

- **Rows** are employees, **columns** are the days of the selected week.
- Each cell shows the hours worked that day. Icons flag things worth a look — alerts, missing clock-outs, offline replays.
- The **Total** column and bottom row give per-person and per-day sums.
- Use the week arrows or the date dropdown to move between weeks.
- **All Employees** filters to a team or person; **Hide employees with no time** collapses empty rows — and Klees remembers this preference per user.

## Drilling into a day

Click any cell to open the **day view** for that person: every entry with clock-in/out times, breaks, GPS points on a map, alerts, notes, and any [clock-out photos](/docs/daily-logs-and-photos/) the worker attached.

From the day view you can:

- **Edit an entry** — adjust clock-in/out times, the job, or breaks.
- **Add a break or lunch** — including the one-tap **+30 min lunch** and **+1 hour lunch** buttons for the common case where a crew forgot to log lunch.
- **Approve** the day, or approve everything with **Approve all** back on the grid.
- **Watch the GPS path** — the embedded map replays the worker's route for the entry.

<Callout type="note" title="Who can edit whose time">
Managers, admins, and supervisors can edit and approve **other people's** time — never their own. Only the **Owner** can edit their own entries. Attempts are blocked server-side, and every change lands in the audit log. See [Self-edit restrictions](/docs/self-edit-restrictions/).
</Callout>

## Adding time manually

Crew forgot to clock in entirely? Add the entry for them:

1. Click **Add Time** (top right).
2. Pick the person, job, date, and the clock-in/out times.
3. Save — the entry is marked as manually added and shows in the audit trail.

## Alerts on entries

Klees flags entries automatically — overtime, missed clock-outs, no-shows, offline punches synced late, GPS anomalies, and **punches made through a VPN or proxy** (which hide the worker's real location — the alert names the VPN provider and where the traffic actually exited). Alerts show on the grid and in the day view; dismiss them once handled. The same alerts feed the [Timesheet Alerts report](/docs/reports/) and the [daily red-flags email](/docs/company-settings/#daily-red-flags-email).

## Approving for payroll

Approving locks the day: the entries stop being editable by the worker and count as payroll-ready. Approve per-day in the drill view, or **Approve all** for the week once you're done reviewing. Approved hours flow into [Reports](/docs/reports/) and your [payroll integration](/docs/payroll/).

<Callout type="tip">
A fast weekly rhythm: Monday morning, open last week → sort out anything flagged → **Approve all** → **Export**. Most teams get review down to minutes once alerts are tuned.
</Callout>
